Event Day Guide & FAQ

Venue Announcement

Specific starting points and loops will be customized depending on your registered category (Cycling tarmac, Skating loop, or Nature Walking route). Registered participants will receive SMS alerts with coordinates.

Safety & Gear Policy

Helmets are strictly required for all cyclists and roller skaters. Route marshals, first aid medics, and water points will be positioned throughout the course.

Photography & Socials

Our media crew will capture high-resolution photos and drone video footage of the ride. All photos will be uploaded to our official social media pages for participants to download and share!
